Its range basis comes from the original Gramian with default relative rank1e-11 / structure1e-10 tolerances.

A separate external wrapper adds the same structural projection on input; no local factor or algorithm is changed.

Thus structural projection does not satisfy the accepted full numerical-range terminal gate on this valid additional-nullity support. Earlier tensor/Latin/nested/two-component cases preserve range at the diagnostic tolerance; weighted output-only actions are not fully symmetric, while the two-sided wrapper repairs that symmetry in these cases. All cases/negatives remain in results-expanded.csv.

This diagnoses eligibility under the planned stricter sparse-terminal gate, not invalidity of existing independently certified LSMR solutions.
